Rules

One writer is God; the other is Flask. The Flask receives text four characters behind. Interrupt for 3 points during the window after a revealed word boundary. Gain a point for each 18 characters the opponent writes. Open, close, or revisit realities with bracket controls for 2 points. Pausing for 6.5 seconds costs a seal. Five seals loses.

Reality brackets and turn seals are different: [ and ] navigate universes. ⟧ marks an interruption or handoff; it doesn't close the current universe. The bracket buttons control the structure; typing a bracket in your story is ordinary text.
